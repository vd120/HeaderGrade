Header and CSP analysis uses heuristic checks — it does not model nonce entropy, effective policy across multiple policies, or browser-specific behaviour. Reputation data comes from the URLhaus and OpenPhish public feeds; a clean result means "not on these lists", not "safe".

What we check

Content-Security-Policy

Restricts script, style and frame sources. The strongest single XSS mitigation.

Strict-Transport-Security

Forces HTTPS and blocks SSL-stripping proxies on repeat visits.

X-Frame-Options

Stops your site being framed for clickjacking.

X-Content-Type-Options

Blocks browsers MIME-sniffing uploads into executable scripts.

Referrer-Policy

Controls what path data leaks to sites you link to.

Permissions-Policy

Turns off camera, mic and geolocation unless explicitly re-enabled.

Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy

Isolates your browsing context against tabnabbing.

Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy

Prevents other origins loading your assets in bulk.

Cookie flags

Verifies Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite on every cookie.

TLS certificate

Real inspection: issuer, validity window, protocol and cipher.
